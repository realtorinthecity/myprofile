Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 2026

This page explains how information you share through this website is handled. Please review it with your legal advisor and edit it to match your business before relying on it.

Information we collect

When you use the "Book a Zoom Meeting" form, you give your name, number of guests, preferred time and preferred date. If you contact us by phone or WhatsApp, we also receive your phone number and the content of your messages.

How we use it

  • To respond to your enquiry and arrange calls or meetings.
  • To share details of properties and offers you have shown interest in.
  • To improve the service we provide.

How it is sent

Form details are sent to us through WhatsApp. This website does not store them on its own servers. WhatsApp's own privacy policy applies to messages sent through its service.

Sharing

We do not sell your personal information. We may share it with developers, partners or agencies only as needed to handle your enquiry, and where required by law.

Your choices

You can ask us to stop contacting you, or to delete the information you have shared, at any time by messaging us on WhatsApp.

Third-party services

This site loads fonts from Google Fonts, which may receive your IP address when the page loads.
